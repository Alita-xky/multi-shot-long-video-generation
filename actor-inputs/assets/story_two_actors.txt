EXT. OLD-TOWN STREET - LATE AFTERNOON

One continuous shot, about seven seconds.

The camera walks slowly down the middle of the cobblestone street. Nine metres ahead, in the middle of the road
and facing us, LEO (young man, loose beige long-sleeved shirt, dark trousers, white sneakers, white bucket hat)
is already dancing: quick footwork, sharp arm swings, turns.

On the right-hand sidewalk a PASSER-BY (older woman in a long grey coat with a brown shoulder bag) walks toward
us, from far down the street to level with the camera, watching Leo as she passes.

At three seconds she calls out "Nice moves!". At five seconds Leo answers "Thanks!" without stopping.

Sound: quiet street, birds, breeze, Leo's sneakers on the stones, her heels on the sidewalk. No music.
