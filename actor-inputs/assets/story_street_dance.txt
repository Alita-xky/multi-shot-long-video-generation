INT./EXT. OLD-TOWN STREET - LATE AFTERNOON

One continuous shot, about seven seconds.

The camera walks slowly down the middle of a quiet cobblestone street toward a young street dancer (LEO) who waits
in the middle of the road about nine metres ahead, facing us. He wears a loose beige long-sleeved shirt, dark
trousers, white sneakers and a white bucket hat.

As soon as the shot starts, LEO dances: quick footwork, sharp arm swings, turns and body isolations, drifting a
little to his left. The camera keeps him framed in the centre and closes the distance to about six metres by the
end of the shot.

Sound: a quiet street, birds, a light breeze in the trees, the squeak and tap of his sneakers on the stones.
No dialogue, no music.
